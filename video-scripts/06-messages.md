## Why two voices, not one

The patient's messages are written in casual Egyptian ("اه", "تمام احجزلي معاه النهاردسهه") — your normal narrator voice fits that. But the AI assistant's replies are formal written Arabic (فصحى-leaning), which reads oddly in a casual dialect voice and oddly in a casual dialect _pace_. Use a second, calmer/more neutral ElevenLabs voice for every 🤖 line — doesn't need to be a "robotic" voice, just distinct enough that a viewer instantly knows who's talking without reading the screen.
